# 🔊 Audio

Your show's sounds live here. Add them through the app's **Library** tab (upload or import), or copy files in and press **↻ Refresh**. Supported formats: wav, mp3, ogg, m4a and flac. Everything is converted once into a standard WAV cache (`_wavcache/`, git-ignored) so playback never depends on codecs.

## The placeholder sounds

These five files were synthesized from scratch for Haunt Director with ffmpeg (filtered noise, sine clusters and envelopes, with no samples or recordings). They are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Use them however you like.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `music-drone-cc0.mp3` | A low, beating dread drone (40 s loop) |
| `amb-wind-cc0.mp3` | Gusting graveyard wind (30 s loop) |
| `amb-storm-cc0.mp3` | Rain hiss with rolling rumble (30 s loop) |
| `sfx-thunder-cc0.mp3` | A thunder crack and long rumble |
| `sfx-stinger-cc0.mp3` | A dissonant scare stinger |

They're here so the example scenes make noise on day one. Real recordings will sound much better!

## Your own audio

Only add audio you have the right to use. Plenty of free, properly licensed horror effects and music are out there; check each license (some ask for credit). Your audio is git-ignored by default, so it won't end up in a fork by accident.
